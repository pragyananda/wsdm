SummitSphere Privacy Policy

Developer: Ved TRUST Foundation · App ID: com.vtrust.summitsphere · Effective 12 November 2026

Summary

SummitSphere is a summit companion app made by Ved TRUST Foundation. You can browse the summit without an account. If you create one, or register for a summit in the app, we collect the information described below to run your account, your registration and your entry to the summit.

We do not sell your personal data, and the app has no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Who is responsible

Ved TRUST Foundation develops SummitSphere and runs the server it uses (summitsphare.vedtrust.org). The organisers of each summit can see the accounts and registrations of that summit's participants. For WSDM 2026, they are the Himalayan Academy of Science & Technology and the summit secretariat.

What we collect when you create an account

  • Your name, email address, mobile number and country, which you give when you sign up.
  • Your password. You choose it, and the app sends it to our server only over an encrypted connection.
  • Profile details you choose to add: professional category, organisation or institution, designation, city, state or region, your interests among the five pillars, a short bio and a LinkedIn profile link.
  • Your selfie, as described under "Your selfie".
  • Your roles at the summit, such as delegate, author or volunteer. The organisers assign them.
  • Records of the consents you give: which version of this policy you accepted, and your agreement to the photo, with the date.
  • The devices you sign in on: a random ID created when the app is installed, whether the device is Android or iPhone, its model, the app version, and when it was last used.
  • The network (IP) address your requests come from, which the server uses to stop repeated attempts, such as too many sign-in codes.

You can browse summit information without giving any of this.

What we collect when you register for a summit

Delegates and authors can register for a summit in the app. When you do, we collect:

  • Your registration details: your category (such as student or faculty), title, the name to print on your badge, your organisation or college, designation or course, department and year of study if you are a student, city, state, and an enrolment or employee number if you choose to give one.
  • Optional details you choose to add: food preference, accessibility needs, whether you need help finding accommodation, an emergency contact's name and mobile number, billing name, address and GSTIN if you need a GST invoice, and your choices about appearing in summit photos and about being contacted by sponsors and exhibitors.
  • Proof of your category: a college email address, which we confirm with a one-time code, or documents you choose to upload, such as a student ID, an ID card or an official letter (PDF, JPEG or PNG).
  • Your fee and its payment status: the amount, the currency, and whether it is paid, waived or refunded. The app does not take payments and never asks for card or bank details.
  • Your e-badge: a registration number and a QR code. The QR code contains no personal information; it is a random code that our server looks up.
  • Check-in records: the day, time and entrance at which your badge was scanned, or you were checked in by name, at the summit.

One-time codes by email

When you sign up, reset your password, verify or change your email address, or verify a college email address, we email you a one-time code. Each code works once and expires after a few minutes. When you change your email address, we also send a security notice to the old address.

How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To confirm your email address and help you recover your account.
  • To keep your account secure. For example, an account is locked for a while after repeated wrong passwords, and other devices are signed out when you change your password or email address.
  • To register you for the summit: to set your fee, check your category, issue your e-badge and, if you ask for one, a GST invoice.
  • To let you into the summit: volunteers and the secretariat scan your badge and compare your photo with you at the entrance.
  • To plan for you at the summit: food, accessibility and accommodation help, and to reach your emergency contact if something happens to you there.
  • To let the summit organisers identify registered participants and manage who takes part, and in which role.
  • To show your profile to other people, only as far as your visibility setting allows.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Your selfie

Most accounts need a photo of the holder's face. You are asked for your consent before the camera opens, and nothing is taken without it. The photo is checked on your phone to make sure it shows a face; that check sends nothing anywhere. It is uploaded only when you tap "Use this photo", with location and phone details removed. It is stored with your account, where the summit organisers can see it and use it to identify you as a registered participant. If you register, it is shown on your e-badge and to the volunteers who check you in. You can replace it at any time from Profile.

Who can see your information

  • You, in the app.
  • The summit organisers and SummitSphere's administrators, through the organisers' panel. This includes your registration details and the documents you upload.
  • Volunteers and the secretariat at the entrance, when they scan your badge or look you up: your name, badge name, photo, registration number, category, organisation, and whether your fee is paid and your category is verified.
  • Sponsors and exhibitors, only if you agreed when registering that they may contact you, and only the contact details the organisers pass on for that purpose.
  • Other participants, only if you allow it. Your profile is visible only to you unless you change "Who can see your profile" to Attendees (other signed-in participants) or Everyone.
  • Services that work for us, such as the email service that delivers your codes, only to provide that service.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to share it.

Permissions

  • Internet: to sign you in and load your account, registration and the summit information.
  • Camera: to take your selfie, and for volunteers and the secretariat to scan e-badges at the entrance. The scanner reads only the QR code; no picture is kept or sent. The camera is used only while one of those screens is open.
  • Files: only when you choose to upload a proof document. The app opens your device's file picker and reads only the file you pick.

Android may also list "view network connections", which the camera scanning component uses to check for a connection; it gives no access to your data. The app does not access your location, contacts or microphone.

What stays on your device

Sessions and sub-tracks you save are stored only on your device. Ved TRUST Foundation cannot see them. If your device backs up app data, for example to your Google or Apple account, they may be included in that backup, under your account's settings.

When you sign in, the app keeps your sign-in in your device's secure storage, so you stay signed in. If you register, it also keeps a copy of your registration and e-badge, so your badge opens at the entrance without a signal. Signing out removes both.

How we protect it

Everything the app sends to our server travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Your sign-in is kept in your device's secure storage, and the short-lived access it uses expires within minutes and is renewed automatically. Your photo, documents and registration are available only to you and to the people described under "Who can see your information". In Account & security you can see every device signed in to your account and sign any of them out.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. A device stays signed in for up to 30 days without being used; after that, you sign in again. We keep your registration, documents and check-in records for as long as the organisers need them to run the summit and issue certificates, and payment and invoice records for as long as tax law requires. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account information, including your photo, your registrations and your documents, except where the law requires us to keep a record.

Your choices

  • View and edit your profile, and choose who can see it, under More, then Profile.
  • Replace your photo at any time from Profile.
  • Change your registration details under More, then My registration & e-badge, and remove a document you uploaded until your category is verified.
  • Change your email address or password, and sign out other devices, under More, then Account & security.
  • To delete your account, open More, then Account & security, then "Delete my account", which prepares an email to contact@vedtrust.org for you to send. You can also write to that address from the email address of your account. We confirm by email when your account has been deleted.
  • To get a copy of your information, correct something you cannot edit in the app, or withdraw a consent, email contact@vedtrust.org from the email address of your account. Because a photo is required for most roles, withdrawing your consent to the photo means closing the account.
  • To remove saved sessions and sub-tracks from this device, open More, then Privacy policy, then "Remove my saved items", or uninstall the app.

Children

SummitSphere is meant for summit participants and is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Links that leave the app

Some buttons open your browser, email, phone or maps app, for example to visit the official summit website or contact the summit secretariat. Anything you share there is handled by those services and by the summit organisers under their own policies, not by SummitSphere.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update it here and in the app before the change takes effect. For important changes, the app asks you to review and accept the new version.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information go to Ved TRUST Foundation at contact@vedtrust.org.
