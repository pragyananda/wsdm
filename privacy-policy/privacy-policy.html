SummitSphere Privacy Policy

Developer: Ved TRUST Foundation · App ID: com.vtrust.summitsphere · Effective November 2026

Summary

SummitSphere does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Sessions and sub-tracks you save are stored only on your device. Ved TRUST Foundation cannot see them. If your device backs up app data, for example to your Google or Apple account, they may be included in that backup, under your account's settings.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Links that leave the app

Some buttons open your browser, email, phone or maps app, for example to visit the official summit website or contact the summit secretariat. Anything you share there is handled by those services and by the summit organisers under their own policies, not by SummitSphere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

In the app, open More, then Privacy policy, then "Remove my saved items". Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If a future version changes what is described here, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to Ved TRUST Foundation: contact@vedtrust.org
